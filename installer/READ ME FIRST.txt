StoryStudio – your AI editing helper for stories, movies and music videos
==========================================================================

What it does
  - Script builder: turns song lyrics or a story into a shot list, with a picture prompt
    (ChatGPT / Nano Banana) and a MiniMax video prompt for every shot, character prompts,
    Suno music prompts, a thumbnail prompt and a title image prompt. No Premiere needed.
  - Add sound effects: reads your story and Premiere timeline, looks at the movie, and makes
    every sound lined up with the picture at the right levels.
  - Fix the audio: cleans up and evens out narration, balances music and effects.
  - Karaoke lyrics: paste the lyrics; every word lights up as it's sung.
  - Edit feedback: checks every cut, the sound and the beat, then watches the movie and
    leaves friendly notes as coloured markers on your timeline.

  Anything that changes a Premiere project is saved as a COPY. Your original is never changed.

Installing (no admin rights needed)
  1. Unzip this whole folder somewhere (for example your Desktop).
  2. Double-click "Install StoryStudio.bat" and leave the window open until it says
     "StoryStudio is installed". The first time takes a while (about 15 GB of downloads:
     Python, the AI engines and models). It works best with an NVIDIA graphics card.
  3. Open StoryStudio from the Desktop or Start Menu.

  If the internet drops during installing, just run "Install StoryStudio.bat" again; it picks
  up where it stopped. A log is saved in your StoryStudio folder (install_log.txt).

Updates
  StoryStudio keeps itself up to date. When it opens it checks for a new version and installs
  it; while it's open it checks every 30 minutes and shows "Update now".

You need
  - Windows 10 or 11 and an internet connection.
  - Claude OR ChatGPT, for the script builder, planning sounds and edit feedback. StoryStudio
    uses your own plan, no extra cost. If you only have ChatGPT: open StoryStudio, click the
    gear (Settings), choose "Set up ChatGPT", then "Sign in to ChatGPT".
    Karaoke and "Fix the audio" work without either.
  - Adobe Premiere Pro, only for the Premiere tasks (sounds, audio, karaoke, feedback).
