StoryStudio – your AI editing helper for stories, movies and music videos
==========================================================================

What it does
  - Script builder: turns song lyrics or a story into a shot list, with a picture prompt
    (ChatGPT / Nano Banana) and a MiniMax video prompt for every shot, character prompts,
    Suno music prompts, a thumbnail prompt and a title image prompt. No Premiere needed.
  - Placing clips: puts your finished clips, with the narration, on a Premiere timeline.
  - Sound effects: plans and makes every sound, lined up with the picture. Afterwards: sounds
    for shots you add, lining the sounds up again after you move shots, changing a sound you
    don't like, and every sound's prompts in one list.
  - Fix the audio: evens out the volume of your clips.
  - Lyrics: karaoke for kids (every word lights up as it's sung), or an elegant lyric video.
  - Checking your movie: a story check (does the story come across?), edit feedback, timing
    and pacing, and "Tell StoryStudio what to fix" in your own words.

  Anything that changes a Premiere project is saved as a COPY. Your original is never changed.

Installing (no admin rights needed)
  1. Unzip this whole folder somewhere (for example your Desktop).
  2. Double-click "Install StoryStudio.bat" and leave the window open until it says
     "StoryStudio is installed". The first time takes a while (several GB of downloads:
     Python, the AI engine, ffmpeg and the speech model). It works best with an NVIDIA
     graphics card.
  3. Open StoryStudio from the Desktop or Start Menu.

  StoryStudio downloads everything it needs by itself. No other program is needed (it doesn't
  need CleanView or CleanListen). A few extra AI models download the first time you use the
  tool that needs them (for example the lyrics timing), so keep the internet on.

  If the internet drops during installing, just run "Install StoryStudio.bat" again; it picks
  up where it stopped. A log is saved in your StoryStudio folder (install_log.txt).

Updates
  StoryStudio keeps itself up to date. When it opens it checks for a new version and installs
  it; while it's open it checks for updates and restarts into a new version by itself.

You need
  - Windows 10 or 11 and an internet connection.
  - Claude OR ChatGPT, for the parts that think (shot lists, planning sounds, the story check,
    feedback). StoryStudio uses your own plan, no extra cost. If you only have ChatGPT: open
    StoryStudio, click the gear (Settings), choose "Set up ChatGPT", then sign in.
    Lyrics, "Fix the audio", lining sounds up and placing clips work without either.
  - Optional: a Fal account (fal.ai) for the best sound effects; without it StoryStudio makes
    sounds on this computer or uses free sound effects.
  - Adobe Premiere Pro, only for the Premiere tasks.
